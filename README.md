# The Winning Factors ⚽📊
## An analysis of modern football metrics in the main European championships

Power BI dashboard developed to analyze which performance indicators are most related to success in European football.

The project investigates whether advanced football metrics can explain the final league standings and identify the factors behind winning teams.

## Project Overview

Football is a complex sport where traditional statistics are often not enough to explain team performance.

This project analyzes modern football analytics metrics to understand how strongly they are connected with league positions and championship victories.

The analysis covers the five major European leagues:

- Bundesliga
- La Liga
- Ligue 1
- Premier League
- Serie A

over the seasons from 2014/2015 to 2021/2022.

## Objectives

The main goals of this project are:

- Compare final league rankings with rankings generated by advanced metrics.
- Identify which metrics better explain team performance.
- Analyze differences between European championships.
- Measure the relationship between performance indicators and final standings.

## Metrics Analyzed

### Expected Goals (xG)
Measures the quality of goal-scoring opportunities based on historical shot data.

### Expected Goals After KeyPasses (xG After KeyPasses)
Evaluates scoring opportunities generated after decisive passes.

### Expected Points (xPoints)
Estimates the expected number of points a team should obtain based on match probabilities.

### Passes Per Defensive Action (PPDA)
Measures defensive pressing intensity.

## Data Analysis

The dataset contains match-level information aggregated by team, season and championship.

Main attributes analyzed:

- Expected Goals
- Expected Goals After KeyPasses
- Expected Points
- PPDA
- Shots
- Shots on target
- Fouls
- Cards

Data preparation included aggregation of match data into team-season statistics. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

## Key Findings

Some relevant insights:

- Teams finishing first in the league generally perform strongly also according to advanced metrics.
- xPoints, xG and xG After KeyPasses show strong relationships with final standings.
- PPDA has a positive relationship with success but is less decisive compared to offensive metrics.
- Different leagues show different tactical patterns and relationships between metrics and rankings.
